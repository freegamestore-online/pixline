## Description

Pixline is a picture puzzle. Each row and column has a set of numbers. Those numbers say how many filled squares appear in that line, and in what runs. Use them to work out which squares to fill, and the picture appears.

- A new puzzle every day, built from the date, so everyone gets the same one.
- Daily puzzles come in 5×5, 7×7 and 10×10. On a phone, pinch to zoom in on a board.
- Tap to fill, tap again to mark a square as empty, tap once more to clear it.
- Undo and redo, with Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Y on a keyboard.
- An optional mistake check that marks wrong squares in red.
- One hint a puzzle, which points to a square the picture needs.
- Clues dim when they are complete. A shimmer marks the moment one completes.
- Finish a picture and it reveals itself, with a burst of confetti.
- Works offline once loaded. Installs to your home screen.
- Light and dark mode, following your system setting.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and each can be solved with line logic alone, so you never have to guess.
